# SampleCo Retail — Shipping Policy
Version: 1.2
Effective date: 2026-05-10
Status: CURRENT

## SP-1 Standard Shipping
Standard shipping normally arrives in 3–7 business days.

## SP-2 Shipping Charges
Original outbound shipping charges are not automatically refundable unless another policy explicitly authorizes them.

## SP-3 Lost Shipments
Orders confirmed lost by the carrier are handled through the shipment-resolution process, not the normal product-return workflow.
