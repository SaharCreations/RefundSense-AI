# SampleCo Retail — Warranty Policy
Version: 1.4
Effective date: 2026-06-15
Status: CURRENT

## WP-1 Manufacturer Warranty
Electronics may include a manufacturer warranty. Warranty service is separate from SampleCo's normal return window.

## WP-2 Defective After Return Window
If an electronic product becomes defective after the 30-day return window, the customer may be directed to warranty service. This does not automatically create refund eligibility.

## WP-3 Warranty and Refund Separation
A valid warranty claim must not be presented as an approved refund unless a separate refund rule applies.
