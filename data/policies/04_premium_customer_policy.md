# SampleCo Retail — Premium Customer Policy
Version: 2.1
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Status: CURRENT

## PC-1 Premium Status
Premium status must be verified from the customer record. The language model must not infer premium status from the customer's wording.

## PC-2 Restocking Benefit
Verified Premium customers have the opened-electronics restocking fee waived.

## PC-3 No Extended Window
Premium membership does not extend the standard 30-day return window.
