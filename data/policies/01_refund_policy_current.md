# SampleCo Retail — Refund Policy
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Status: CURRENT

## RP-1 Standard Return Window
Most products may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery.

## RP-2 Opened Electronics
Opened electronics may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery.
A 15% restocking fee normally applies.

## RP-3 Premium Customer Exception
Premium customers do not pay the 15% restocking fee on opened electronics.

## RP-4 Final-Sale Items
Items explicitly marked FINAL SALE at purchase are not refundable unless defective on arrival.

## RP-5 Refund Method
Approved refunds return to the original payment method. The refund amount must never exceed the amount actually paid for the item.

## RP-6 Damaged-on-Arrival Exception
A product reported damaged on arrival within 7 calendar days of delivery may be refunded or replaced even when the item would otherwise be non-refundable.

## RP-7 Missing Policy Coverage
If the current policy does not address a requested situation, the system must not invent a rule. The request must be escalated for human review.
